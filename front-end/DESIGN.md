## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative scientific urgency and life-saving clinical clarity. Designed for precision oncology researchers, translational bioinformaticians, clinical oncologists, and patient advocates querying genomic knowledge graphs, the aesthetic combines high-density clinical rigor with mission-driven conviction.

The visual grammar balances structured scientific modernism with clinical warmth:
- **Mission Conviction:** Anchored by deep crimson/magenta tones representing urgent patient-first translational discovery.
- **Scientific Architecture:** Grounded in deep midnight slate structural frames, high-contrast crisp white canvas surfaces, and hairline structural borders.
- **Biomedical Clarity:** Information density is handled with clear data hierarchies, monospace-augmented genomic coordinates, and specialized multi-omic categorical accents.
- **Clinical Accessibility:** High contrast, legible typography, and reduced visual noise allow rapid interpretation of complex survival curves, variant call formats, and therapeutic targets.

## Colors

The palette directly addresses clinical confidence and computational genomics:

- **Primary (`#8B0053`):** The signature vibrant crimson/magenta. Used for critical brand expressions, primary scientific query actions, primary graph highlights, and life-critical interactive elements.
- **Secondary (`#182830`):** Deep midnight slate. Serves as the structural framework, high-contrast typography base, navigation shells, and genomic table header rows.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488`):** Precision teal. Assigned to genomic cohorts, clinical trial enrollments, multi-omic linkage markers, and verified scientific citations.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp, clinical-grade off-white base layer that eliminates eye fatigue under dense data visualization while preserving stark contrast against midnight slate text (`#0F172A`).
- **Named Semantic Colors:**
  - `brand-burgundy-deep` (`#60023E`): Hover, active, and high-impact states of primary actions.
  - `peach-tint` (`#FFE1CC`): Soft clinical badge background for spotlighting translational insights and patient advocacy notices.
  - `accent-cyan` (`#06B6D4`) & `accent-indigo` (`#4F46E5`): Categorical palette for structural DNA/RNA variants, expression assays, and cytogenetics.
  - `accent-amber` (`#D97706`): Flags variants of uncertain significance (VUS) and safety cautionary markers.
  - `status-pathogenic` (`#DC2626`) & `status-benign` (`#16A34A`): Standardized ACMG/AMP clinical pathogenicity classifications.

## Typography

The typography pairings unify technological precision with unyielding editorial authority:

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides a modern, structural, and subtly computational presence for patient summaries, genomic locus indicators, and cohort titles.
- **Body & Editorial (Inter):** High-efficiency neutral typeface engineered for complex medical text, long-form clinical trial protocols, and AI reasoning streams.
- **Genomic & Tabular Metadata (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced type allocated to genomic HGVS coordinates (e.g., `chr14:q32.33`, `BRAF V600E`), allele frequencies, statistical p-values, and database identifiers (COSMIC, ClinVar, PubMed).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display concurrent knowledge graph canvases, copilot chat panels, and multi-track variant browsers.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum 32px (`2rem`) page margin. Multi-pane panels support dual 6-column views or a 4-column AI prompt drawer paired with an 8-column interactive network node diagram.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Data tables enable horizontal scrolling with fixed chromosome/gene ID columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) edge margins. Complex graphs collapse into ranked faceted lists and interactive touch drawers.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Strictly tied to an 8px base grid (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`), maintaining disciplined alignment across complex analytical dashboards.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy dropped shadows in favor of a **clinical tonal-stacking model combined with crisp structural outlines**:

- **Surface 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F8FAFC` base application background.
- **Surface 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Zero ambient shadow in rest state.
- **Surface 2 (Interactive Floating / Graph Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and a subtle, high-clarity drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(24, 40, 48, 0.08)`.
- **Surface 3 (Clinical Drawers, Modals & Copilot Floating Palettes):** `#FFFFFF` bordered by `#94A3B8` with an elevation shadow of `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(24, 40, 48, 0.16)`.
- **Knowledge Graph Node Layers:** Multi-tiered depth achieved via semi-translucent backdrops (`rgba(24, 40, 48, 0.85)` for dark inspection tooltips) with a 4px blur backdrop to isolate genomic insights without losing spatial context.

## Shapes

The geometric framework favors an authoritative, controlled **Soft (`1`)** radius schema:

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, scientific tooltips, and data cells) utilize a crisp 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- Cards, data-viz frames, and modal containers use an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius.
- Tags, genomic variant chips, and status badges use pill borders (100px) exclusively when communicating discrete, non-actionable entity tokens (e.g., `KRAS`, `t(11;14)`).
- This low-radius approach evokes clinical instrumentation, diagnostic monitors, and laboratory precision rather than consumer software casualness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid crimson `#8B0053` background with pure white text, 4px corner radius, 12px vertical and 20px horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#60023E`.
- **Secondary (Structural):** Deep midnight slate `#182830` fill with white text, used for committing computational workflows or exporting cohort datasets.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), text colored `#182830`. Hover applies a `#F1F5F9` background and `#8B0053` border.

### Genomic & Entity Chips
- Small, compact badges (24px height) set in `JetBrains Mono` at 12px.
- Genomic alterations: Soft `#FFE1CC` background with `#60023E` text and a subtle 1px border (`#FDBA74`).
- Pathway entities: Pale cyan tint with `#0E7490` text.
- Interactive filter chips include an inline dismissal cross (`×`) and display active/inactive states through border thickness (1px to 2px solid `#8B0053`).

### Cards & Data Panels
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Header bars feature an optional 2px top accent line in `#8B0053` or `#0D9488` to designate data domains (e.g., Clinical History vs. RNA-Seq Expression).
- Padding is fixed at `1.25rem` (20px) to maximize scientific data density while maintaining scannability.

### Input Fields & Knowledge Graph Query Prompts
- Single-line inputs: 40px height, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), white fill, with active focus state defined by a 2px outline in `#8B0053` and zero offset.
- Research AI Prompt Input: Multi-line rich input with an embedded monospaced pill counter, token preview, and a flush `#8B0053` query execution action button docked at the bottom-right corner.

### Tables & Data Grid
- Headers: `#182830` background with crisp white text (`#F8FAFC`), uppercase 11px monospaced typography with 0.05em letter spacing.
- Rows: Alternating zebra rows of `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`. Border dividers: 1px `#E2E8F0`.
- Numeric and coordinate fields automatically align right and render in tabular-numeral fonts.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- 16px square (or circle) with 1.5px border (`#94A3B8`). Selected state features a full fill of `#8B0053` with a crisp white checkmark or center pip.
